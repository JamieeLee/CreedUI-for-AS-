Boss timer
The themed bar with every boss, a countdown to the next one, and their health while up. Replaces the game's run clock.
Boss and mini-boss bars
Health bars under the boss timer with the real numbers. Replaces the game's boss bars.
Damage numbers
Your hits as calm numbers with the skill's icon. A spell hitting 3+ enemies adds up into one number a second.
Your frame
Your health with the real numbers, shield, energy/mana/rage, class meter and extra lives. Replaces the game's player frame.
Target frame
Your target's health with the real numbers, and whether it's a boss, guardian or elite. Replaces the game's target frame.
Party frames
Up to four friends with health bars in their class's color and the real numbers, their mana/energy/rage, and Dead or Offline when they are. Click one to target them. Replaces the game's party frames.
Skill bar
Your abilities with their cooldowns, your neck, rings and trinkets (hover for what they give), and your XP. Replaces the game's bar.
Kills, gold and cache
Kills, gold and the next cache's price with little icons, docked in the skill bar's panel (drag it away with /creed move). The cache lights up when you can afford it. Replaces the game's kills and gold box.
Minimap
A metal ring in the map's colors, the local time on its foot and the zone's name under it. The mouse wheel zooms.
Clock on the minimap
A little box on the minimap ring's foot with your local time. Hover it for the run's time and the time to the final boss.
Chests on the minimap
Every cache on the field as a gold chest on the minimap, or on its rim pointing the way when it's farther. A new one rings as it drops; they glow while your gold pays for one.
Lairs on the minimap
Every guardian's lair as a skull token on the minimap: bronze while its guardian sleeps, red while it fights, grey once empty with the time until the next guardian comes. The one to head for rings slowly. Hides the game's red flag.
Damage meter
Damage Done, Healing Done, Boss Damage and Party DPS with icons, per second and shares; Reset counts again in this window only. Drag the title to move it, the corner to resize. Replaces the game's meter window.
Luck
Your luck on a bronze medallion, and the chance of a Rare, Epic or Legendary card as three gems (1 in N); luck you gain shows on the medallion. Drag its sides to widen it, its corners to size it; the clover on the minimap's edge shows or hides it.
Chat
No scroll buttons: the mouse wheel scrolls (Shift for the top or bottom), an arrow brings you back down. A glass behind the lines while you read or type, and a typing box in the timer's style.
